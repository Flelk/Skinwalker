# Changelog

## 5.0.0
- Much less often: no lures for the first 3 minutes of a level, then one every 2.5 to 5 minutes instead of every 25 to 60 seconds.
- Nobody gets taken again within 5 minutes of being taken.
- The Swap waits at least 45 seconds before attacking, and can't lead straight into another Swap.

## 4.0.0
- It only goes after someone with no teammate within 15 meters, and gives up if one catches up.
- Copied voices are quieter and don't carry as far.
- You never hear it using your own voice.

## 3.0.0
- It's never invisible now. It always looks like a player, including while it wanders and when it grabs you.

## 2.0.0
- Removed personal info from the mod's files. The settings file is now flelk.skinwalker.cfg, so settings go back to their defaults.

## 1.0.0
- First version.
